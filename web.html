Filaments in real lensing data

Each glowing line runs along a ridge of the DES Y3 mass map, where mass piles up between emptier regions. The map is measured from how DES galaxy shapes are distorted by gravitational lensing.

    How this was made
    1. Start from the 100 KaRMMa posterior samples of the DES Y3 mass map and stack its four tomographic bins.
    2. Smooth to ′ and flood the map downhill from every low point, merging shallow dips.
    3. Where two of these low regions meet is a ridge of mass, which is a filament in projection.
    4. Repeat on all 100 samples. A line is bright only when there is mass along it and most samples draw it too.
